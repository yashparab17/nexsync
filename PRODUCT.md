# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (Tauri 2 desktop shell, React 19)

## Users
Small teams of developers and students (2–10 people) who co-edit notes, code and tasks and want to own their files. They work in long sessions, often with collaborators connected live.

## Product Purpose
Local-first, peer-to-peer workspace for notes, code, files, tasks and kanban. A workspace is a folder on disk; sharing is a direct encrypted connection between two copies of the app. Success: people trust their files stay theirs and can see at a glance who is connected and what changed.

## Positioning
No account, no cloud, no server: collaboration by pasteable ticket, files as plain files.

## Constraints (confirmed)
- Keep square corners (radius 0).
- Keep the teal brand colour and existing logo assets.
- Keep Plus Jakarta Sans as the UI typeface.
- Redesign scope for this pass: design foundation (tokens, buttons), Welcome, Dashboard. Other pages inherit tokens.
